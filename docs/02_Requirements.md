## Business Streams

Business Streams are admin-configurable and represent the service area.

Examples:

- Mold Design
- Product Design
- Automation Design
- Software Development
- Manufacturing Engineering

Each project belongs to one Business Stream.

## Task Types

Task Types are admin-configurable and belong to a Business Stream.

Examples under Mold Design:

- Mold Design
- Engineering Change
- Feasibility
- Design Review
- BOM Creation
- Drawing Update

A project may have one or more Task Types.

Task Types are maintained by Admin users.
