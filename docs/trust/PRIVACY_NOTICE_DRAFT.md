# Privacy Notice (Draft — SaaS / design partners)

**Status:** Draft for legal review  
**Controller contact:** privacy@prosohm.com (update before publish)

## What we collect

Account identity, authentication events, project and timesheet operational data, optional HR modules you enable, support communications, and technical logs.

## Why we process

To provide ProTrack, secure accounts, improve reliability, meet contractual obligations, and comply with law.

## Legal bases (EU/UK where applicable)

Contract, legitimate interests (security, product improvement), and consent where required.

## Sharing

Subprocessors listed in `SUBPROCESSORS.md`. No sale of personal data.

## Retention

Active tenant data for the subscription term. Audit/activity logs per Security policy `audit_retention_days` (default 365). Backups per ops schedule.

## Rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection where applicable — contact the Controller email above.

## Security

Tenant isolation (ORM filters; optional PostgreSQL RLS), hashed API keys, SSO option, access controls, and audit logging.

## Changes

Material updates posted with version date; continued use after notice constitutes acceptance where permitted.
