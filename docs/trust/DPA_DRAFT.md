# Data Processing Agreement (Draft)

**Status:** Draft for legal review — not executed  
**Parties:** Prosohm / ProTrack (“Processor”) and Customer (“Controller”)  
**Related:** Privacy notice, subprocessors list

## 1. Subject matter

Processor provides the ProTrack Engineering Operations Platform and processes Customer Personal Data solely to deliver the Service.

## 2. Nature and purpose

Hosting, authentication, project/timesheet/HR operational records, support, and security monitoring as configured by Customer.

## 3. Duration

Term of the subscription / design-partner agreement, plus retention as stated in the privacy notice and audit retention policy.

## 4. Types of data / data subjects

Typical: employee identity and work data (name, email, role, timesheets, performance/exit records as enabled). Customer determines categories.

## 5. Obligations of Processor

- Process only on documented instructions.  
- Confidentiality for authorized persons.  
- Appropriate technical and organizational measures (access control, tenancy isolation, encryption in transit, backups).  
- Assist with data subject requests and breach notification without undue delay (target: 72 hours after Processor becomes aware).  
- Delete or return data at end of service (subject to legal hold).

## 6. Subprocessors

Listed in `docs/trust/SUBPROCESSORS.md`. Material changes notified in advance where feasible.

## 7. International transfers

Documented if Customer data leaves the agreed region; SCCs or equivalent as required.

## 8. Audit

Customer may request evidence of controls annually (SOC reports when available, questionnaires, isolation test summaries).
