ProTrack is a web-based project tracking and resource planning system
for Prosohm.

The platform will initially support Mold Design projects and be
extensible to future business streams such as Product Design,
Automation Design, Manufacturing Engineering and Software Development.

Core capabilities include:

- Project Tracking
- Milestone Management
- Timesheets
- Resource Planning
- Dashboard & Reporting
- User Management

The platform shall support multiple business streams through configurable:

- Business Streams
- Task Types
- Milestone Templates

No workflow shall be hardcoded to Mold Design.
